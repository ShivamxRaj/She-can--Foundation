About Us

Our Story

Empowering women and young girls through health support and computer education.

She Can Foundation was established in 2021 as a registered NGO (under the Indian Society Act, 1860) with a vision to build a world where lack of basic hygiene and educational access doesn't stop girls from realizing their potential.

Under the leadership of founder Reeta Mishra, the foundation works actively in school communities and rural areas to deliver essential sanitary napkin kits, run menstrual health workshops, and establish free digital training centers. By pairing direct physical support with job-ready technical courses, we help women achieve absolute self-reliance.

"Together we can build a future where every young woman is healthy, educated, and digitally independent."
What We Do

Our Three Pillars of Impact

We tackle systemic barriers holding women and girls back, combining basic health support with advanced educational and vocational skills.

Menstrual Hygiene

Distributing sanitary napkins and hosting period health awareness workshops. We provide kits to ensure no girl misses school due to a natural bodily process.

Education Access

Preventing dropouts by providing school kits, learning resources, and counseling support to girls from economically vulnerable families.

Women Empowerment

Delivering digital literacy, vocational training courses, and professional mentorship networks to help young women step confidently into employment.

Leadership Spotlight

Reeta Mishra

Founder & President, She Can Foundation

With decades of committed social activism in education and community development, Reeta Mishra established She Can Foundation to dismantle the barriers preventing young Indian girls from achieving self-reliance. Her holistic model addresses critical health obstacles through hygiene kits while training women in digital tools to forge concrete paths into technological fields.

"We believe that when you empower a woman, you empower an entire generation. Technology and health resources are the greatest equalizers of our time, and we are committed to making sure every woman has a seat at the table."
